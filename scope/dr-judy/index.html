In detail

What each part is, and what it does for you.

Everything below is in Sales Engine: Full Business. The Lead Magnet Test holds the keyword funnel, the free chapter, email capture and posting.

Shorts in your locked look

What it is: your best lines as short videos. Each opens on a yellow question, carries your WTFreud! lettering, and closes on the same end card. Why it matters: people recognise you in one second, and every video ends on your website and phone number.

Daily posting on four platforms

What it is: we post for you on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts, with a title, description and hashtags on each one. Why it matters: your videos reach people every day, and nobody on your side has to post.

The keyword funnel

What it is: each video invites viewers to comment one word, for example HEAL. Your page then sends them a private message with your free chapter, automatically. Why it matters: a viewer becomes a contact in seconds, while they still feel what you said.

Your free chapter as the gift

What it is: the free sample chapter your site already offers, and the first chapter of the new book for Healing Childhood Wounds. Why it matters: people try your work first, so the step to a program feels smaller.

Email capture and your list

What it is: everyone who takes the gift leaves an email address, saved in a list that belongs to you. Why it matters: platforms can change overnight. Your list is the one audience you own.

Email follow-up, written for you

What it is: a welcome series for new contacts, then one email a week. We write them in your voice, and you approve each one. Why it matters: most people don't buy on day one. The emails stay with them until they're ready.

Free-consultation booking

What it is: a calendar on your consultation page, so people pick a time themselves and answer a few questions first. Why it matters: you told us you can't manage the requests. This way only booked calls reach you.

Websites tuned to sell

What it is: one clear button per page, lighter opening videos, reviews where people decide, and an email sign-up on every site. Why it matters: more of the visitors you already have take the next step.

Book launch for February

What it is: a waitlist page, the first chapter as a gift, and a run of launch videos and emails for Healing Childhood Wounds. Why it matters: the book opens to people who are already waiting for it.

Your show, twice a month

What it is: we edit each long episode, post it, and announce it to your list and your pages. Why it matters: each episode also gives us a month of new Shorts.

Webinars

What it is: we set up the sign-up page, the reminders and the room, run the tech on the day, and send the follow-up emails. Why it matters: you teach live to people who chose to be there.

The Gift House

What it is: short videos of the house once we have footage, plus an inquiry form with what scouts need to know. Why it matters: location scouts can send a request at any hour, not only by phone.

A weekly report

What it is: one short page: what we posted, which videos did best, how many new emails came in, and what we do next. Why it matters: you always see what your money is doing.
