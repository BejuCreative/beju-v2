Every part in detail

How each part works, step by step

Each card says what we do, what your part is, and gives one example.

01

Shorts in your look

What we do

We watch each long video from start to end and mark the lines people will relate to most. Each line becomes a video of 30 to 50 seconds. It opens on a yellow question while you start talking. Your WTFreud! lettering stays on screen, and it closes on your end card.

Your part

Approve the first video in each style. After that, you only tell us when something feels wrong.

Example

From your talk on why we love people who keep hurting us, one Short opens on “Why do we love people who hurt us?” It ends on your line about being blueprinted that way.

02

Daily posting

What we do

We keep a calendar with one video a day. Each post gets its own title, description and hashtags, written for that platform. Your show days and your webinar dates get their own posts.

Your part

Give us access once, through your assistant. We never ask for a password by email.

Example

Monday's video goes out on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts, each with its own title and hashtags.

03

Your show

What we do

We edit the full episode, post it, and tell your email list and your pages that it's out. Then we cut the same episode into the next weeks' Shorts.

Your part

Record as you do now, and share the file.

Example

One recording becomes a full episode, about a dozen Shorts, and one email to your list.

04

The keyword funnel

What we do

This is how a viewer becomes a contact. Each video invites people to comment one word. When someone comments, your page sends them a private message within seconds. The message offers your free chapter. The chapter is only for people who follow you, so it asks for a follow first, and then for their email address. So every new contact is also a new follower.

Your part

Choose the word, and approve the message once.

Example

“Comment HEAL and I'll send you the first chapter, free.” A viewer comments HEAL at 11 pm. One minute later they follow you, they have your chapter, and you have their email address.

05

Welcome emails

What we do

A new contact gets five short emails in their first two weeks. The first one delivers the chapter. The next ones share your teaching and explain what your program covers. The last one invites them to the program or to a free consultation. We write them in your voice.

Your part

Approve the five emails once. Then they run for every new contact.

Example

Day 1: the chapter. Day 3: one idea from your work. Day 6: a story. Day 9: what the program covers. Day 13: the invitation.

06

Weekly email

What we do

Once a week, your whole list hears from you. We take that week's strongest video, turn it into a few written lines, and add a link to your program, your show or your next webinar.

Your part

Approve it with one reply.

Example

A Thursday email with one idea and one link. It takes two minutes to read.

07

Consultation booking

What we do

Today your free consultation is a form, and someone has to call each person back. We add a calendar to your site. People pick a time themselves, and reminders go to them before the call.

Your part

Open the hours that work for you. You can change them whenever you like.

Example

You open Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2 to 4. A new contact books Thursday at 2:30, and it lands in your calendar.

08

Sites that convert

What we do

Your sites keep their look. We fix what stops visitors from taking the next step, and we build email sign-up into each site. We already found three quick fixes. The quiz button on your main site leads to a missing page. Your free chapter downloads without asking for an email address. And the Healing Childhood Wounds site still carries the other site's title.

Your part

Choose how far we go on each site. Nothing changes without your OK.

Example

On healingnarcissisticwounds.com, the free chapter asks for an email address first. From then on, every download adds a person to your list.

09

Book launch

What we do

Your new book comes out in February, so the list should start growing now. We build a waitlist page with the first chapter as the gift. Then we make videos on the book's ideas. In launch week, everyone on the waitlist gets your emails.

Your part

Send us the chapter, the cover and your launch date.

Example

Someone joins the waitlist in December. On launch day they get an email with the link to buy.

10

Webinars

What we do

When you want to run one, we do everything around it: the sign-up page, the invitations to your list, the reminders, the room, and the tech on the day. Afterwards we send the replay and the follow-up emails.

Your part

Example

A one-hour class on healing after narcissistic abuse. At the end you present your program, and the replay goes to everyone who signed up.
