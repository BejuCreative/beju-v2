03How it works

From one video to a program sale

Here is the whole path, with one of your own videos as the example.

1

Someone sees your video

A Short opens on your question in yellow: “Why do we love people who keep hurting us?” They stop scrolling to hear the answer.

2

They comment one word

The video says: comment HEAL for the free chapter. They comment. Within seconds, your page sends them a private message. The chapter is for your followers, so the message asks them to follow you first.

Hi, it's Dr. Judy's team. Your free chapter is ready. It's for followers, so tap Follow first. Then type your email address, and we'll send it.

3

They follow you and join your list

They follow you and type their email address, and the chapter arrives. You gain a follower, and their address is in a list that belongs to you.

4

Your emails stay with them

Over two weeks they get five short emails in your voice. After that, one email a week.

5

They take the next step

They buy Healing Narcissistic Wounds or Healing Childhood Wounds. Or they book a free consultation, at a time you opened.

Every comment grows your audience twice.

Each person who comments becomes a follower and a name on your email list. And every comment tells the platform that people care, so it shows the video to more people.

07Every part in detail

How each part works, step by step

Each card says what we do, what your part is, and gives one example.

01

New videos every day

What we do

We make two or three new Shorts for you every day. We watch each long video from start to end and mark the lines people will relate to most. Each line becomes a video of 30 to 50 seconds. It opens on a yellow question while you start talking. Your WTFreud! lettering stays on screen, and it closes on your end card.

Your part

Approve the first video in each style. After that, you only tell us when something feels wrong.

Example

From your talk on why we love people who keep hurting us, one Short opens on “Why do we love people who hurt us?” It ends on your line about being blueprinted that way.

02

Posted on all your channels

What we do

We post your videos for you on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook, every day, on a calendar you can see. Each post gets its own title, description and hashtags, written for that channel. Your show days and your webinar dates get their own posts.

Your part

Give us access once, through your assistant. We never ask for a password by email.

Example

Monday's video goes out on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook, each with its own title and hashtags.

03

Your show

What we do

We edit the full episode, post it, and tell your email list and your pages that it's out. Then we make the next weeks' Shorts from the same episode.

Your part

Record as you do now, and share the file.

Example

One recording becomes a full episode, about a dozen Shorts, and one email to your list.

04

The keyword funnel, for followers

What we do

This is how a viewer becomes a follower and a contact. Each video invites people to comment one word. When someone comments, your page sends them a private message within seconds. The message offers your free chapter. The chapter is only for people who follow you, so it asks for a follow first, and then for their email address. The comments also tell the platform that people care, so it shows the video to more people.

Your part

Choose the word, and approve the message once.

Example

“Comment HEAL and I'll send you the first chapter.” A viewer comments HEAL at 11 pm. One minute later they follow you, they have your chapter, and you have their email address.

05

Welcome emails

What we do

A new contact gets five short emails in their first two weeks. The first one delivers the chapter. The next ones share your teaching and explain what your program covers. The last one invites them to the program or to a free consultation. We write them in your voice.

Your part

Approve the five emails once. Then they run for every new contact.

Example

Day 1: the chapter. Day 3: one idea from your work. Day 6: a story. Day 9: what the program covers. Day 13: the invitation.

06

Weekly email

What we do

Once a week, your whole list hears from you. We take that week's strongest video, turn it into a few written lines, and add a link to your program, your show or your next webinar.

Your part

Approve it with one reply.

Example

A Thursday email with one idea and one link. It takes two minutes to read.

07

Consultation booking

What we do

Today your free consultation is a form, and someone has to call each person back. We add a calendar to your site. People pick a time themselves, and reminders go to them before the call.

Your part

Open the hours that work for you. You can change them whenever you like.

Example

You open Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2 to 4. A new contact books Thursday at 2:30, and it lands in your calendar.

08

You built your sites, and they look like you. We keep that look and fix what stops visitors from taking the next step: a button that leads nowhere, a page that loads slowly, a download that asks for nothing. We also build email sign-up into each site. Section 08 lists the fixes site by site.

Your part

Choose how far we go on each site. Nothing changes without your OK.

Example

On healingnarcissisticwounds.com, the free chapter asks for an email address first. From then on, every download adds a person to your list.
